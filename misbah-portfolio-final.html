The thinking behind the work

Good websites make the next step obvious.

A potential customer should quickly understand what you do, where you work and how to contact you. Your website and local search presence should make that decision easier.

I'm Misbah, a web designer and local SEO specialist focused on helping service businesses build a more useful digital presence. I bring together WordPress design, local keyword research and on-page SEO so the site looks considered and communicates clearly.

My approach is practical: understand the business, organize the information around what customers are looking for, and make the enquiry path easy to follow.
